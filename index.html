Our Story

Brewed with Passion, Served with Care

Brew & Bloom started as a small idea between two friends who believed that a great cup of coffee could change the mood of a whole day. Since 2018, we've grown into a neighbourhood staple — a place where baristas know your order, the music is always just right, and the wifi never lets you down.

We source our beans directly from farms in Nepal and Ethiopia, ensuring fair prices for growers and exceptional taste for you.
